## What it measures

ScienceWorld evaluates an agent's ability to complete science tasks in a simulated environment. The score is the mean of the environment's per-task scores.

Each method studies **100** training tasks over **20** rounds. The **100** `dev` tasks are reserved for choosing a checkpoint, using the highest verified dev score and breaking ties in favor of the earlier round. Each chosen checkpoint is evaluated once on **100** held-out `test` tasks.
