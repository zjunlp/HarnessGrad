## Deviations from the method it ports

4 deliberate difference(s), recorded in `methods/sica_ci/method.json`:

- The platform owns candidate measurement and the stopping budget; normal returns record the selected historical round as step.incumbent_round when its comparison bound has an available CI.
- An unavailable comparison CI leaves incumbent_round unspecified rather than asserting a fallback kept round.
- The selected historical round is recorded and named in the prompt, but its source snapshot is not restored for editing.
- When no iteration clears the bound, this port keeps the bound holder; upstream `select_base_agent` falls off the end of its loop and returns None.
