## Deviations from the method it ports

5 deliberate difference(s), recorded in `methods/harnessx/method.json`:

- Rollback restores the staged historical-best tree and declares incumbent_round, but grading is delayed until the next method call.
- The upstream shipping validator is not executed; version selection alone does not implement its preflight checks.
- The optional cost penalty uses platform tokens rather than upstream USD.
- The passed-count guard has a legacy aggregate fallback and accepts breaches with unavailable counts.
- Edit generation uses the shared editor, with JSON pre-registration rather than the resident HarnessX meta-agent and journal workflow.
