## Deviations from the method it ports

8 deliberate difference(s), recorded in `methods/dream_rsi/method.json`:

- The replay simulator is absent.
- Each round's studied score replaces the paper's normalized Pareto hypervolume; no subtree max and no average over replay worlds is formed.
- The priced quantity N is the request count (Eq. 1), not kilo-tokens; beta1/beta2 stay unspecified (neither paper version publishes a value) and kilo-tokens are still recorded
- The denominator k is n_trials rather than completed replay decision rounds, and with b2=0.0 the port carries no parallelism term while v2 penalizes serial policies.
- The offline M-revision phase is absent: every revision is paid for.
- The proposer edits a harness rather than a dedicated executable exploration policy with the shared online/replay batch interface.
- Argmax is over recorded round proxies; the arithmetic check does not establish the paper's replay or deployment guarantee.
- The port implements v1's linear Eq. (1), which v2 superseded with the appendix's Pareto objective.
