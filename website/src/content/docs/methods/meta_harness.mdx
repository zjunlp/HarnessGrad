## Deviations from the method it ports

5 deliberate difference(s), recorded in `methods/meta_harness/method.json`:

- History depth is the newest 12 states, not every prior candidate.
- The gate cannot smoke-test.
- A bounded two-stage batch replaces the upstream agent-written candidate manifest.
- Design is an endpoint JSON call, rather than the upstream trajectory-reading subagent.
