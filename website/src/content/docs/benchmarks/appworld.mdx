## What it measures

A simulated world of apps — e-mail, calendar, contacts, a file system, shopping, a music player — behind REST-like APIs. A task is a natural-language goal that must be reached by calling those APIs, and it counts as solved only when the official evaluator finds the world state in the target state: a right-looking answer that never touched the world is a failure.

Study on **90** ids from the official `train` split, scored every round on the training tasks the method was allowed to learn from; each round is also scored on the official `dev` split (**57** tasks), which never enters the search and is the pool a round is chosen on. `test_normal` (168) and `test_challenge` (417) are the eval side: measured once, on the round that was chosen.
