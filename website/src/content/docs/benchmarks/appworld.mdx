## What it measures

A simulated world of apps — e-mail, calendar, contacts, a file system, shopping, a music player — behind REST-like APIs. A task is a natural-language goal that must be reached by calling those APIs, and it counts as solved only when the official evaluator finds the world state in the target state: a right-looking answer that never touched the world is a failure.

Study on **90** ids from the official `train` split, scored every round on the training tasks the method was allowed to learn from; each round is also scored on the official `dev` split (**57** tasks), which never enters the search and is the pool a round is chosen on. `test_normal` (168) and `test_challenge` (417) are the exam: measured once, on the round that was chosen.

**Re-measured rounds.** `dgm` r4; `harnessx` r4, r6; `tthe` r6, r7, r8, r9; `rrsi` r1, r2, r3, r4. Those rounds report a state byte-identical to an earlier round (the method submitted no change, or its own validity gate rejected the proposal), so they are measurements of an already measured state rather than new ones. They stay in the record — they show what the gate cost — and are not counted as rounds learned.
