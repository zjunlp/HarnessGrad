# Website i18n review

The English homepage remains at the original root, and Chinese is under `/zh/`. Only visits to the root negotiate: a saved manual choice takes priority, then the first supported browser language (English or Chinese), otherwise English. Deep links, including explicit Chinese links, do not redirect. Manual switching preserves search and hash; `hg-lang=en` prevents negotiation overriding an explicit English home choice even if storage is unavailable.

The home story and illustrations, navigation, getting-started page, and news/blog index descriptions are bilingual. Both homepages retain the exact HarnessGrad brand title. Starlight generates Chinese routes for untranslated documentation; these display a Chinese notice above English content. Benchmark results, methods, contribution pages, and the published news article remain English. The Chinese journal index says articles are in English and links to the originals. Blog currently has no posts. Existing evidence and result datasets were not changed.
